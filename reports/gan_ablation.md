## Verdict

- Weight 0.3, rare days: MAE of P50 changes by +7.6, +18.7, +27.1 per seed (mean +17.8): higher (worse).
- Weight 0.3, all days: MAE of P50 changes by +1.9, +4.6, -2.1 per seed (mean +1.5): mixed.
- Weight 0.1, rare days: MAE of P50 changes by +7.4, +19.7, +2.7 per seed (mean +10.0): higher (worse).
- Weight 0.1, all days: MAE of P50 changes by +1.4, +0.4, -9.1 per seed (mean -2.4): mixed.

A change is only a real gain if it is lower for every seed and clearly larger than the spread between seeds. Otherwise the GAN rows did not help.
